TIMELINE

March 25, 1971
Operation Searchlight begins in East Pakistan, leading to crackdown on Bengali population.
March 26, 1971
Sheikh Mujibur Rahman declares the independence of Bangladesh.
April 1971
Pakistani military operations intensify in East Pakistan.
August 1971
India and the Soviet Union sign a treaty of friendship and mutual assistance.
November 22, 1971
Battle of Garibpur: India captures the Boyra salient in East Pakistan.
December 3, 1971
Pakistani Air Force attacks Indian airfields, leading India to declare war.
December 3–16, 1971
Armed conflict occurs on both the eastern and western fronts.
December 6, 1971
Bhutan recognizes Bangladesh.
December 16, 1971
Pakistani forces in East Pakistan surrender, marking the creation of Bangladesh.
December 16, 1971
The war officially ends, with India achieving victory.
